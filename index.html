I am a chemical oceanographer and marine biogeochemist specializing in ocean trace-metal cycling, biogeochemical processes, and numerical modeling.

My research combines observations, numerical models, and data synthesis to investigate how redox reactions, organic ligands, sedimentary inputs, particle dynamics, and ocean circulation regulate the distribution and biological availability of iron and other nutrients.

I develop and apply regional and global ocean biogeochemical models to connect chemical processes with marine ecosystems, carbon cycling, and climate change.

Research Interests

Iron Biogeochemistry

Redox chemistry, organic ligand binding, scavenging, sedimentary iron inputs, particle dynamics, and biological uptake.

California Current System

Iron transport and cycling along continental margins and connections between ocean biogeochemistry and marine ecosystems.

Marine Ecosystems

Interactions between iron and heterotrophic bacteria, zooplankton, phytoplankton, and higher trophic levels.

Ocean Models & Observations

Regional and global biogeochemical models, observational datasets, model intercomparison, and model–data comparison.

Marine Carbon Cycling

Connections between trace-metal cycling, carbon cycling, benthic–pelagic coupling, and climate-change feedbacks.
