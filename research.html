Research

My research combines observations, numerical models, and data synthesis to investigate chemical and biogeochemical processes controlling trace-metal cycling and marine ecosystems.

Iron Biogeochemistry

I investigate redox chemistry, organic ligand binding, scavenging, sedimentary iron inputs, particle dynamics, and biological uptake.

Iron Cycling along Continental Margins

I investigate how sedimentary inputs, particle dynamics, redox chemistry, organic ligands, and ocean circulation regulate transport of iron from continental margins to the open ocean.

California Current System

I combine regional ocean modeling and observations to investigate physical and biogeochemical processes controlling iron distributions along the U.S. West Coast.

Iron and Marine Food Webs

I investigate how iron availability and recycling influence heterotrophic bacteria, zooplankton, phytoplankton, and higher trophic levels.

Global Iron Model Evaluation

I evaluate ocean biogeochemical models using observations and large-scale data synthesis, including comparisons with GEOTRACES observations.

Marine Carbon Cycling

I am interested in interactions between trace-metal cycling, marine carbon cycling, benthic-pelagic coupling, and climate-change feedbacks.
